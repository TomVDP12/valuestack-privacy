ValueStack

My card isn't in the list. Can I still track it?

Yes. On the My Cards tab, tap + Add a card manually below the search box. Enter the card's name and annual fee, then use + Credit and + Benefit on the card to add each statement credit and perk yourself. Manually added cards work exactly like built-in ones.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

Everything stays on your device. There is no account and no server, so nothing syncs between an iPhone and an iPad, and deleting the app deletes its data. See the privacy policy for details.

A card's fee or credits are wrong.

Built-in card details are starting estimates — issuers change terms often. Tap the card, then Edit to correct the annual fee, or tap edit on any credit to fix its amount or how often it resets. Your changes are kept. If you spot something out of date, email us and we'll update it for everyone in the next release.

How do reminders work?

ValueStack schedules reminders on your device for credits you haven't used yet — 7 days before a monthly credit resets, 14 days for quarterly, and 30 days for half-yearly and yearly ones. When several fall on the same day they're grouped into a single notification. Checking a credit off cancels its reminder. You can turn notifications off any time in iOS Settings.

What does "Earns its fee" mean?

It compares what you've actually checked off this year against the card's annual fee. The bar is red until you've recovered half the fee, orange up to the full fee, and turns green with an "Earns its fee" badge once your captured value passes it.

I updated the app and my card's details didn't change.

Cards keep the details they had when you added them, so app updates never overwrite your edits. To pick up refreshed built-in data for a card, remove it and add it again.

How do I erase everything?

Tap the Private badge or the ⓘ icon at the top of the app, then Reset all data. This clears every card, check-off, and referral link from the device and cannot be undone.

Ember: Endless Night

I bought a map but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and tap Restore purchases. Maps are tied to your Apple Account rather than to one device, so this is also what to do on a new phone, after reinstalling, or if a purchase was interrupted. If the row still says Failed, check you're signed in to the App Store with the account you bought on.

Which maps cost money?

Night Canal is free and complete — all three difficulty tiers, its own high scores. Orbit and Amazonia are $0.99 each, bought separately; there's no subscription and nothing else to buy. Every map plays identically: the physics, the wind and the scoring are the same everywhere, so a paid map is a different place to play, not an easier or a better one.

Where are my high scores kept? Do they sync?

On your device only. There's no account and no server, so scores don't move between an iPhone and an iPad, and deleting the app deletes them. Purchases are the exception — those live with your Apple Account and come back with Restore purchases. See the privacy policy.

How do I reset my high scores?

Settings → Reset high scores, then tap the row a second time to confirm. It clears every map and every difficulty at once and can't be undone. It does not affect maps you've bought.

The game is silent, and the haptics aren't working.

Ember has no audio at all — that's deliberate, so it can be played anywhere without headphones. Haptics can be switched off in Settings; if they're on and you still feel nothing, check that iPhone Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics is enabled.
