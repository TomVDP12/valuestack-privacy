ValueStack — Privacy Policy

Effective July 18, 2026
ValueStack collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

What the app stores, and where

ValueStack is a personal tracker for credit-card perks and statement credits. The cards you add, the credits and benefits you check off, and any referral links you paste are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. This information is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app makes no network requests of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, ValueStack does not:

  • require or offer an account, login, or registration;
  • collect personal information, identifiers, or usage analytics;
  • use third-party tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • access your contacts, location, camera, photos, or any other device data beyond its own local storage.

Note that ValueStack never asks for real card numbers or any financial account details — it only tracks card names and perk values that you enter.

Your data, your control

Because your data lives only on your device: deleting the app deletes all of its data, and the app's About screen includes a "Reset all data" action that erases everything it has stored. There is no copy anywhere else — which also means we cannot recover your data for you, and it does not sync between devices or survive a reinstall.

External links

The Discover tab's "Apply" buttons open card issuers' public websites (or a web search, or a referral link you yourself provided) in your browser. Those websites are operated by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children

ValueStack is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled — for example, by adding optional sync — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be clearly described here.
