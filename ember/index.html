Emberwick Relaxing arcade

Flick an ember down a dark canal and light the night.

The wind is shown before every throw, so a miss is always yours to fix. Miss three and the night ends. One thumb, portrait, no sound needed — an attempt takes two to four seconds, which is about the length of a red light.

Get it on the App Store iPhone · iOS 16+

Free. One optional $2.99 unlock opens every other place.

Three rules, and that is the whole game.

Flick to throw

Drag back and let go. Direction sets where it lands, length sets how far. There is nothing else to learn.

Read the wind

It is on screen before you commit, and it is locked the moment you release. The game never changes it mid-flight.

Three misses

Then the night ends and you see two numbers: your best, and this run. One tap starts another.

Three places to light.

What it does not do.

  • No ads. Not between runs, not anywhere.
  • No grind. No currency, no upgrades, no energy to wait on.
  • Nothing you buy improves the throw. The unlock buys places to play, and the physics is identical in all of them.
  • No account, and no servers. It has never sent anything anywhere.
  • Works with no signal. Which is the point — it was built for the train.

Help

I bought the unlock and my maps are locked
Open the gear on the title screen and choose Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also brings them to a new phone.
My best scores are gone
Scores are kept on the device and nowhere else. Deleting the app or moving to a new phone clears them. That is the trade for having no account.
The game has no sound
Correct. There is none to turn on. It was designed to be played with the ringer off, in public, with one hand.
Haptics, or turning them off
The gear on the title screen. They are on by default and off is remembered.
Anything else
tomvdp@valuestackapp.com — a real person reads it.

Privacy, briefly

Emberwick collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no server to send anything to. Your best scores, the place and difficulty you last chose and your haptics setting are stored on the device by the app itself.

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees a payment detail. Full policy: privacy policy.
